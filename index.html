REF 01 The problem we remove

Middlemen tax you twice.

Getting an import license in Bangladesh is slow and expensive — so most small businesses buy through middlemen instead. Those middlemen charge a 10–30% markup and rarely show you the real costs.

KiAnben replaces both barriers. Members pool their orders into shared batches that ship legally under KiAnben's own import licenses. We run sourcing, payments, customs and delivery end-to-end for a flat 1% commission, with every cost itemized before you commit.
